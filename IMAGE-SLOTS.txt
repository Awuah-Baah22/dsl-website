IMAGE AND FILE SLOTS
====================
Every place a photo, logo, video or PDF goes, page by page.
Put a file at the path shown (same name, same folder) and it appears on the site.
If a file is missing, the site shows a neat placeholder (or the company name for logos), so nothing breaks.
Many of these (hero pics, home logos, What we do photos) are already in your OLD images folder:
copy your old images folder into this site first, then add the rest.
Tip: keep photos under about 400 KB (export JPG at 80% quality, around 1600 px wide).
