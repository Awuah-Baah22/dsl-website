Our Commitment to ESG: Environmental, Social & Governance Responsibility

At Dos Santos Limited, we believe that responsible business is sustainable business. Our ESG strategy reflects our commitment to ethical practices, environmental stewardship, and social responsibility—ensuring we create long-term value for our stakeholders, employees, community, and the planet.

Visit Our ESG Initiative
Our belief

Responsible business is sustainable business.

Our ESG commitments rest on three pillars, each with clear policies and practical actions on every project.

Our approach

Three pillars, one standard

Environmental, social and governance commitments that guide every project we deliver.

E

Environmental Responsibility

We are dedicated to reducing our environmental impact and promoting sustainable practices across all aspects of our operations. Our Environmental Policy outlines our approach to:

Reducing carbon footprint

through energy efficiency and renewable energy initiatives

Minimizing waste

and promoting recycling and responsible disposal

Complying with all environmental legislation

and striving for continuous improvement

Training employees

to understand and take responsibility for their environmental impact

Our aim is to operate in a way that preserves natural resources for future generations.

S

Social Responsibility

We recognize the importance of nurturing a positive social impact both within and outside our organization. Our social commitments include:

Diversity, equity, and inclusion

in hiring, development, and leadership

Fair labor practices

and a safe, healthy workplace

Community engagement

through charitable programs and local partnerships

Transparency

and ethical conduct in all relationships

We believe that empowered employees and thriving communities are key to sustainable growth.

G

Governance & Ethics

Strong corporate governance is the foundation of trust and accountability. We uphold the highest standards of integrity in our operations by:

Diverse and transparent board structure

Maintaining a diverse and transparent board structure

Clear ethical codes of conduct

Enforcing clear ethical codes of conduct

Risk management and compliance

Prioritizing risk management and compliance

Shareholder rights and financial transparency

Promoting shareholder rights and financial transparency

Our governance framework ensures we are accountable to our stakeholders and compliant with regulations.
